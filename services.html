Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our organic farming techniques and partnership programs.

How long does it take to convert a conventional farm to organic?

Typically, the transition period takes between 24 to 36 months depending on local certification standards. During this period, our agronomists utilize bio-char, cover cropping, and biological inoculants to rapidly flush synthetic residues from your soil.

Are organic fertilizers as effective as synthetic NPK fertilizers?

Yes! While synthetic chemical fertilizers provide a rapid, short-lived chemical rush that degrades soil microbiome, our organic bio-fertilizers feed both the plant and the soil ecosystem. This yields stronger cell walls, higher drought resilience, and equal or superior harvest tonnage over time.

How does STACKLY help farmers sell their organic crops?

STACKLY operates a direct supply chain network connecting partner farms with wholesale distributors, grocery chains, and eco-conscious direct consumers, guaranteeing fair pricing and eliminating middleman markups.
