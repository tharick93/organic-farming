Contact & Advisory FAQs

Do you provide on-site farm visits for soil diagnostics?

Yes. Our field agronomists conduct on-site soil sampling and topographical scans for farms exceeding 10 acres. You can request a visit via the contact form above.

How quickly will I receive my soil test results?

Standard lab spectrometry reports are generated within 3 to 5 business days after sample receipt, accompanied by a custom bio-nutrient plan.
